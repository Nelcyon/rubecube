How to play

Goal: make every side of the cube one solid color. Press Scramble to mix it up, then put it back.

Turning pieces

  • The buttons around the cube act on its front, the side facing you.
  • The buttons below the cube move a column, the buttons on its left move a row, and the buttons on its right twist the front, middle or back slice like a steering wheel (as seen from the front).
  • Left click turns it the way the big arrow points. Right click turns it the other way.
  • Point at any button first: the pieces it will move get a pink outline, so there are no surprises.

Seeing other sides

Use Turn whole cube to spin, tip or roll it (right click goes the other way). This never counts as a move.

No rush

Every action is a single click or a single key. Nothing needs to be held, dragged or pressed together, and nothing times out. Made a mistake? Undo takes it back, as many times as you like. Your game is saved in this browser automatically.

Keys and QuadStick

With a QuadStick in mouse mode, puff on the center tube to click a button and sip on it to turn that button the other way. In keyboard mode, move the yellow frame, sip right (Enter) to press the framed button and soft sip center (Ctrl-C) to press it the other way. Change any key under Keys.

Keys

Each action takes a single key or mouse button, never a combination you have to hold. Keys act once per press: holding a key or a doubled signal won't turn the cube twice.
