Algorithms

An algorithm is a short recipe of turns. Pick one to learn it one turn at a time. Your game is kept aside and comes back exactly as it was when you close this.

How to play

Goal: make every side of the cube one solid color. Press Scramble to mix it up, then put it back.

Turning pieces

  • The buttons around the cube act on its front, the side facing you.
  • Each button sits right against the edge it turns: the buttons along the left edge move a row (the right edge if you choose the mirrored layout under Button side in Settings), the buttons along the bottom edge move a column, and the buttons along the bottom of the right side twist the front, middle or back slice like a steering wheel (as seen from the front).
  • Left click turns it one way. Right click turns it the other way.
  • Point at any button first: the pieces it will move get a pink outline, so there are no surprises.

Seeing other sides

The outer buttons with the double arrows turn the whole cube: Spin on the left, Tip at the bottom left and Roll on the right (right click goes the other way). This never counts as a move.

No rush

Every action is a single click or a single key. Nothing needs to be held, dragged or pressed together, and nothing times out. Made a mistake? Undo takes it back, as many times as you like. Your game is saved in this browser automatically.

Letters on the buttons

Each turn button also shows its letter in standard cube notation: R right, L left, U up (top), D down (bottom), F front, B back, M, E and S for the three middle slices, and x, y, z for turning the whole cube. Each button shows the turn and its reverse; a mark (′) means the other way round. On the turn buttons the letters replace the words; turn the letters off in Settings to see words instead.

Challenge

Challenge mixes the cube with a few turns and gives a par: the fewest moves that solve it. Solve it in exactly par for gold; a few more earn silver or bronze. Solved counts the scrambled games you solved yourself; challenges and games finished with Solve it with me don't count.

Algorithms and the solver

Algorithms teaches a recipe one turn at a time on the real cube. Solve it with me walks you through solving your own cube, stage by stage. In both, the button for the next turn glows green: press it yourself, or press Next move. Undo and Redo step back and forward too.

Keys and QuadStick

With a QuadStick in mouse mode, puff on the center tube to click a button and sip on it to turn that button the other way. In keyboard mode, move the yellow frame, sip right (Enter) to press the framed button and soft sip center (Ctrl-C) to press it the other way. Change any key under Keys.

★ Challenge

The cube is mixed with only a few turns. Solve it in as few moves as you can. Par is the fewest moves it can possibly be solved in.
